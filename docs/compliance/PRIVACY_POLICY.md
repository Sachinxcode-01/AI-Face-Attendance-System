# SmartFace Attendance — Institutional Privacy & Biometric Data Policy

> **Effective Date**: January 1, 2026 | **Version**: 2.1 (Enterprise Multi-Tenant Edition)  
> **Applicable Entities**: Rural Engineering College Hulkoti, IIT Dharwad Campus, and Partner Educational Institutions.

---

## 1. Scope & Core Privacy Commitment
SmartFace Attendance ("the Platform") provides automated facial-recognition and multi-factor attendance recording for educational institutions. We operate under the foundational principle of **Privacy by Design & Default**:
- **Volatile Processing of Scan Imagery**: Facial photographs submitted during enrolment or at scanning kiosks are received directly into volatile memory (`multer.memoryStorage()`), transformed into 512-dimensional numerical feature embeddings, and promptly discarded from memory without writing raw images to persistent server disk.
- **No Third-Party Commercial Exploitation**: Student and staff biometric vectors are never sold, rented, monetized, or shared with commercial advertising or surveillance brokers.
- **Multi-Tenant Logical Sandboxing**: Biometric records, attendance logs, and student profiles are logically isolated by institutional tenant identifier (`college_id`), enforced by centralized RBAC and mandatory tenant scoping on database queries.

---

## 2. Categories of Data Collected
1. **Identification & Profile Data**: Full name, student roll/USN number, institutional email address, department, semester, class section.
2. **Sensitive Biometric Vector Templates**: 512-dimensional floating-point numerical embeddings generated by deep neural networks (ArcFace ResNet-100). These mathematical representations constitute sensitive biometric data under GDPR Article 9 and DPDP Act 2023 and are protected with high-grade security controls.
3. **Attendance Telemetry**: Timestamp of check-in, classroom session identifier, verification modality (`face`, `qr`, `offline_sync`), camera kiosk location, and ambient lighting quality score.
4. **Security & Access Logs**: Client IP address, user-agent, device security posture (root/jailbreak status), and failed authentication attempts.

---

## 3. Legal Grounds for Processing (GDPR & DPDP Act 2023)
- **GDPR Article 9(2)(a)**: Explicit consent provided by the data subject (or legal guardian for minors under age 18) for processing sensitive category biometric data prior to enrollment.
- **Educational Legitimate Interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f))**: Maintenance of accurate classroom attendance records for academic credit accreditation and examination eligibility.
- **India Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023**: Processing strictly for specified educational purposes with verifiable consent.

---

## 4. Biometric Data Security & Storage Architecture
- **Encryption in Transit**: TLS 1.3 encryption with HTTP Strict Transport Security (`max-age=15552000; includeSubDomains`) and SPKI Certificate Pinning on mobile endpoints.
- **Encryption at Rest**: AES-256 database encryption across all MongoDB Atlas collections and Hive mobile offline queues.
- **Revocable Access Control**: Access limited to authenticated institutional API keys and role-based permissions (`student`, `teacher`, `admin`, `super_admin`).

---

## 5. Data Retention & Automatic Purge Schedule
| Data Category | Active Retention Period | Archival / Backup | Purge Mechanism |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Biometric Vectors** | Active Academic Degree Duration | Standard database backup cycle | Hard deletion via administrative purge API upon student departure or erasure request |
| **Daily Attendance Scans** | Current Academic Year (12 months) | 3 years encrypted cold storage | Aggregated to percentage summaries; raw logs purged |
| **Security Intruder Logs** | 90 days rolling window | Zero cold storage | FIFO truncation at 500 records |
| **User Sessions & Tokens** | 24 hours (JWT access) / 7 days (Refresh) | Zero persistence | Automated MongoDB TTL index expiry |

---

## 6. Student & Guardian Rights (Right to Erasure & Deletion Workflow)
Students and parents maintain absolute rights to:
1. **Inspect Stored Records**: View all attendance stamps and enrollment status directly in the Mobile and Web portal.
2. **Withdraw Biometric Consent**: Opt out of facial recognition at any point and transition to alternative attendance modalities (Dynamic QR / manual teacher roll-call) without academic penalty.
3. **Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten")**: Submitting an erasure request triggers hard deletion of the user's `BiometricProfile` 512-d embedding records, revocation of active device tokens, and anonymization of historical attendance logs.
4. **Request Submission Route**: Erasure and consent withdrawal requests may be initiated through the Institutional Admin Portal (`DELETE /management/users/:id/biometrics`), via the Mobile App settings under "Biometric Privacy", or by contacting the Institutional Data Protection Officer via `privacy@smartface.pro`.
